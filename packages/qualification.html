What we do for you

  • Map your qualification against New Zealand's framework and identify the correct recognition route for your trade or profession
  • Build your complete evidence file: certificates, transcripts, syllabi, employer letters and experience records

  • Deliver your recognition outcome with a plain-language explanation of what it means for your pathway

Why this matters so much

Recognition is the foundation stone. Employers take you more seriously with a recognised qualification, and the trades pathway generally requires one. Applications fail most often for administrative reasons — a missing syllabus, an uncertified translation, an inconsistent name spelling across documents. Those are exactly the failures a managed process prevents.

Timeline

Document collection typically takes 2–4 weeks (mostly waiting on your institutions and employers); assessment bodies then take their own processing time. Starting while you're still employed in the Gulf — with easy access to your employers and attesting offices — is dramatically easier than starting later.

Honest boundaries

Our fee covers coordination and management. Third-party fees — assessment body charges, translation costs, courier and attestation fees — are charged at cost and quoted to you upfront before you commit. Recognition outcomes are decided by the assessing body, not by us, and this service is administrative support, not immigration advice.
