What we prepare for you

  • A personalised document checklist for your visa type, issued under the adviser's direction
  • Employment evidence file: offer letter, employment agreement details, employer information — organised the way Immigration New Zealand expects
  • Work experience records: reference letters from your Gulf employers, drafted-for-signature templates your managers can easily approve
  • Qualification and identity documents: collation, certified copies and translation coordination for non-English documents
  • Health and character requirements: guidance on approved panel physicians in the Gulf and police clearance procedures for every country you've lived in
  • Final consistency review — names, dates and job titles matching across every document, the small errors that cause big delays
  • A complete, submission-ready file delivered to your Licensed Immigration Adviser

Why Gulf applications need special care

Gulf employment documents come with their own complications — attestation requirements, Arabic-language contracts, company letterheads that change with every restructure, and police clearances from multiple countries of residence. We work with these documents every day, so nothing surprises us and nothing gets missed.

How it works

1. Enquire. Tell us your situation — visa type, job offer status, family members included.

2. Receive your quote. A fixed price based on your file's complexity. No hourly billing, no surprises.

3. We build your file. You upload documents to a secure folder; we chase, check, translate and organise.

4. Your adviser takes over. The completed file goes to the Licensed Immigration Adviser, who reviews it and manages your application.

Honest boundaries

BelongNZ's role is document preparation and administration, carried out under the direction of a Licensed Immigration Adviser. Advice about your eligibility, visa strategy and the application itself comes from the adviser — and Immigration New Zealand alone decides the outcome. If you haven't yet had an eligibility assessment, start with the Licensed Assessment so you invest in an application that's realistic.
