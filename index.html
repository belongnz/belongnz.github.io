Questions

Asked by almost everyone. Answered honestly.

Can I get New Zealand residence directly from the Gulf?

For most skilled workers and tradespeople, no — and we won't pretend otherwise. The 2026 pathways generally require a period of skilled work in New Zealand first, usually on an employer-sponsored work visa. The realistic journey is: job offer → work visa → NZ work experience → residence application. Our packages support every stage of that journey.

I'm a tradesperson without a degree. Do I have a chance?

The Trades & Technician pathway was designed exactly for people like you — qualified tradespeople with real experience. Whether your specific trade, qualification and experience fit is a question for a Licensed Immigration Adviser, which is what our assessment package is for.

Who actually assesses my eligibility?

A Licensed Immigration Adviser — a professional licensed and regulated under New Zealand law, with a searchable public licence number. BelongNZ handles your booking, documents, communication and support; the immigration advice itself always comes from the LIA.

Do you guarantee a visa?

No. Nobody can — all decisions are made by Immigration New Zealand. What we can promise: an honest, licensed assessment of your position, transparent pricing, and that we will never take your money for a pathway we don't believe is realistic for you.

Can my family come with me?

New Zealand's skilled pathways are built with families in mind — partners and dependent children are typically included in residence planning. Your adviser will map your whole family's situation in your assessment, not just yours.
