# BelongNZ — Photo Guide (simple version)

Your homepage now has three photo slots. Until you add photos, they show elegant green panels — nothing looks broken. Adding photos takes 10 minutes.

## Step 1 — Get free, legal photos

Use **unsplash.com** or **pexels.com** — both are free for commercial use, no payment, no credit required. **Do not use Google Images** (most results are copyrighted).

Search these terms and pick one photo for each slot:

| File name (must match exactly) | What to look for | Search terms to try |
|---|---|---|
| `nz-city.jpg` | NZ city skyline / working life | "Auckland skyline", "Wellington city", "Auckland harbour" |
| `nz-family.jpg` | Family / kids / warm life moment | "family beach walking", "family park sunset", "children playing outdoors" |
| `nz-nature.jpg` | Iconic NZ landscape | "New Zealand mountains lake", "Milford Sound", "Lake Tekapo" |

Tips for choosing:
- Pick **landscape (wide)** photos, not tall ones.
- Warm, hopeful, real-life photos beat postcard-perfect ones — your audience should see *their* future family in them.
- Avoid photos with visible brand logos or recognisable individual faces used prominently (safer commercially).

## Step 2 — Rename and resize

1. Rename each downloaded photo to exactly: `nz-city.jpg`, `nz-family.jpg`, `nz-nature.jpg` (lowercase).
2. Size: photos from these sites are often huge (5–10 MB) which makes your site slow. Before uploading, shrink them at **squoosh.app** (free, in your browser): open the photo, set width to **1600px**, quality ~75%, download. Aim for under 400 KB each.

## Step 3 — Upload to GitHub

1. Go to your repository → open the `assets` folder → open the `img` folder.
2. **Add file → Upload files** → drag your three photos in → **Commit changes**.
3. Wait 2 minutes, refresh your site. The photo band appears between the packages and the journey section — on both English and Sinhala pages automatically.

## Want to change a photo later?

Upload a new file with the same name — GitHub replaces the old one automatically.

## What NOT to do

- ❌ Don't use photos from Google Images, Facebook, or other websites — copyright risk for a commercial site.
- ❌ Don't use photos of identifiable clients without written permission.
- ❌ Don't add more than these three photos to the homepage — speed matters more than decoration, especially for Gulf mobile users.
